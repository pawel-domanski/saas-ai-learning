
### What is Unsupervised Learning?

It's like giving a child a box of colorful blocks without instructions:
- System receives data without labels
- Must find patterns and similarities on its own
- Creates groups or discovers structures

### How Does It Work in Practice?

**Example: Customer Segmentation in a Store**
1. System receives data about thousands of customers (age, purchases, visit frequency)
2. No information about who is a "good" or "bad" customer
3. System finds groups of similar customers on its own
4. Discovers e.g.: "young online shoppers", "seniors preferring stores", "bargain hunters"
5. You interpret what these groups mean for business

### Main Tasks:

**🎯 Clustering (grouping)**
- Dividing data into natural groups
- Example: grouping news articles by topic
- No predefined categories

**🔍 Anomaly Detection**
- Finding elements that don't fit the rest
- Example: unusual transaction on a bank account
- System learns what is "normal"

**📊 Dimensionality Reduction**
- Simplifying complex data
- Example: image compression while preserving essential features
- Allows viewing complex data in a simpler way

### Where Do We Encounter This?

**Marketing:**
- Automatic customer grouping
- Discovering new market segments

**Security:**
- Detecting unusual network behavior
- Identifying potential threats

**Science:**
- Grouping genes with similar functions
- Discovering new types of stars in astronomy

**E-commerce:**
- "Customers who bought this also bought..."
- Discovering shopping trends
