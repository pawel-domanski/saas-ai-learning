## 5 most important things about AI:

1. **AI is a tool** - it is used to solve problems that require intelligence.
2. **AI doesn't have to think like a human - it can achieve goals in other ways.
3. **All current AI is narrow AI** - each system has a specific, limited application.
4. **AI is already everywhere** - you use it every day, often unconsciously.
5. **The definition of AI is changing** - what used to be AI is now just a function of a computer.

### What's next?
The definition of AI will evolve as the technology evolves. It is worth tracking the changes and understanding how AI will affect our lives.
