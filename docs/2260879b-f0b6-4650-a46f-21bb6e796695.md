**General AI** (Artificial General Intelligence, AGI) is a system capable of performing ANY intellectual task at human level or better. It's the difference between a calculator and a child - the calculator calculates faster, but the child can learn everything.

**How AGI would look in practice:**
- Would conduct meaningful conversations on any topic
- Would learn new skills without special training
- Would understand context, emotions, jokes, metaphors
- Would adapt to new situations independently
- Would possess consciousness and plan long-term
- Would combine knowledge from different domains

**Key features of AGI:**
- **Versatility** - universal application of intelligence
- **Transferability** - moving knowledge between domains
- **Self-awareness** - understanding own capabilities
- **Adaptability** - adjustment without reprogramming
- **Creativity** - creating new solutions
- **Context understanding** - deep comprehension of situations

**Human analogy:**
AGI would be like an intelligent assistant from science fiction movies - not only would it execute commands, but it would understand their meaning, anticipate needs, and propose innovative solutions.

**Current state:**
AGI doesn't exist. It's a goal, dream, or challenge for the future. All current AI systems, even the most advanced ones, are narrow AI.
