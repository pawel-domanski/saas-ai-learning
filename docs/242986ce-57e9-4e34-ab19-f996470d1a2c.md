### Key features of AI:
- **Broadest scope** - encompasses all methods mimicking intelligence
- **Different approaches** - from rigid rules to flexible learning
- **Long history** - developing since the 1950s

### Examples of AI (that don't use machine learning):

**Expert systems:**
- Program diagnosing diseases based on symptoms
- Works according to pre-programmed medical rules
- Doesn't learn, but mimics expert knowledge

**Problem-solving algorithms:**
- Chess programs (early versions)
- Route planning algorithms
- Logical reasoning systems

### Sports analogy:
AI is like the term "sport" - a very broad concept encompassing:
- Team sports (soccer, basketball)
- Individual sports (tennis, running)
- Winter, summer, water sports...

Similarly, AI encompasses different "disciplines": rule systems, machine learning, expert systems, and many others.

### Everyday AI examples:
- **Home thermostat** - automatically regulates temperature
- **ABS system in cars** - prevents wheel locking
- **Scientific calculator** - solves complex equations

### Conclusion:
AI is an umbrella covering all attempts to create intelligent systems - from the simplest to the most complex.
