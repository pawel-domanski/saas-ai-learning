The main idea behind AI

Definition:
Artificial intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows machines to do things that require human intelligence. It's like "teaching" computers to think, or at least mimic human thought processes.
Here's an example:
When you chat with a voice assistant like Siri or Alexa, you're using AI. The system is like your own personal assistant. It understands what you're saying, interprets your questions, and provides you with the answers — all on its own!
Here are the main points to keep in mind:

I know it might sound like something out of a sci-fi movie, but AI isn't just a concept — it's a real, living technology that's part of our world today.
It's not required to think like a human, but it still manages to achieve similar results, which is really impressive!
You know, it's funny how this thing is already a part of our everyday lives.
