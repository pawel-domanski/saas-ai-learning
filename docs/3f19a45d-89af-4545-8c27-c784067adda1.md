### Hybrid Approaches

**Semi-Supervised Learning**
- Combines supervised and unsupervised
- Uses small amount of labeled data + lots of unlabeled
- Example: Face recognition system learning from 100 labeled and 10,000 unlabeled photos

**Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback**
- Humans suggest good strategies
- System learns faster and safer
- Example: ChatGPT trained through human feedback

**Meta-Learning**
- "Learning how to learn"
- System learns to quickly adapt to new tasks
- Example: AI that after learning chess, learns checkers faster

### Breakthrough Examples

**GPT and Language Models**
- Use all three approaches!
- Unsupervised: learn language structure from texts
- Supervised: learn specific tasks
- Reinforcement: adjust responses to human preferences

**Autonomous Cars**
- Supervised: sign and object recognition
- Unsupervised: detecting unusual situations
- Reinforcement: driving optimization

**DeepMind's AlphaFold**
- Protein structure prediction
- Combines physics, supervised learning, and self-learning
- Breakthrough in medicine and biology
