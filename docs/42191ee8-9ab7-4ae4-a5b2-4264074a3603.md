### When to choose traditional AI (rule-based systems):

**Best when:**
✅ You know the problem rules exactly  
✅ You need decision transparency  
✅ You have little data  
✅ Problem is stable (rules don't change)

**Use examples:**
- **Home thermostat** - simple temperature rules
- **Payroll system** - clear salary calculation rules
- **Industrial automation** - known production procedures

**Disadvantages:**
❌ No adaptation to new situations  
❌ Requires manual programming of every rule

---

### When to choose machine learning:

**Best when:**
✅ You have structured data  
✅ You need to understand decision logic  
✅ You want to solve a specific, well-defined problem  
✅ You have moderate computational requirements

**Use examples:**
- **Real estate price prediction** - based on area, location, construction year
- **E-commerce recommendation systems** - "customers like you also bought..."
- **Bank fraud detection** - transaction pattern analysis

**When to avoid:**
❌ Data is very unstructured (raw images, text)  
❌ Problem requires understanding context and nuances

---

### When to choose deep learning:

**Best when:**
✅ Working with unstructured data (images, text, sound)  
✅ You have huge datasets  
✅ You need highest accuracy  
✅ You have large computational resources

**Use examples:**
- **Medical diagnostics** - X-ray image analysis
- **Autonomous cars** - real-time object recognition
- **Voice assistants** - natural speech understanding
- **Language models** - ChatGPT, translation

**When to avoid:**
❌ You have little training data  
❌ You need to explain every decision  
❌ Limited budget or computational resources

---

### Practical selection table:

| Criterion | Traditional AI | Machine Learning | Deep Learning |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Data amount** | Small | Medium | Very large |
| **Cost** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Transparency** | High | Medium | Low |
| **Accuracy** | Medium | Good | Highest |
| **Implementation time** | Fast | Medium | Long |

---

### Real company examples:

**Netflix uses all three:**
- **Traditional AI:** Basic content filtering
- **ML:** Recommendation system based on ratings
- **DL:** Image analysis for creating thumbnails

**Bank uses different levels:**
- **Traditional AI:** Automatic transfers according to rules
- **ML:** Suspicious transaction detection
- **DL:** Speech recognition in call center

### Golden rule:
**Start with the simplest solution that solves your problem!**

Don't use deep learning if a simple rule system is enough.

### Future:
The greatest potential lies in **combining** all approaches - hybrid systems using strengths of each level.
