### What is a Neural Network?

**A neural network** is a computer system that mimics how the human brain works:

- Human brain = billions of connected neurons
- Artificial network = thousands of connected "artificial neurons"

### Why Do We Mimic the Brain?

The brain is amazing at:
- Recognizing faces in a crowd
- Understanding speech despite noise
- Learning from experience
- Finding patterns in chaos

Neural networks try to recreate these abilities in a computer!

### Basic Idea

Imagine a neural network like a factory:
1. **Input**: Raw materials (data)
2. **Processing**: Production line (layers of neurons)
3. **Output**: Finished product (result)

### Example: Recognizing Digits

Task: Teach a computer to recognize handwritten digits (0-9)

**Traditional approach**: 
- Programmer writes hundreds of rules ("if it's a circle, it might be 0 or 6 or 8...")
- Complicated and unreliable

**Neural network approach**:
- Show the network thousands of digit examples
- Network discovers patterns on its own
- Can recognize even illegible handwriting!

### Where Do We Encounter This?

Neural networks work behind the scenes when:
- 📱 You unlock your phone with your face
- 🗣️ You talk to a voice assistant
- 📧 Gmail filters spam
- 🎬 Netflix recommends movies
- 📸 Instagram applies filters

They're everywhere, though we often don't realize it!
