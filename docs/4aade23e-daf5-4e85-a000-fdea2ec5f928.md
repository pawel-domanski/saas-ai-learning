**Comparison in specific applications:**

**SPAM FILTERING**
- *Rule-based system*: "If email contains word 'Viagra' → spam"
- *Learning system*: Analyzes thousands of labeled emails and learns subtle spam patterns

**CAR DRIVING**
- *Rule-based system*: "Red light → stop, STOP sign → stop"
- *Learning system*: Observes thousands of hours of human driving and learns to react to unexpected situations

**MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS**
- *Rule-based system*: "Fever + cough → consider flu"
- *Learning system*: Analyzes thousands of medical cases, finding subtle disease patterns

**LANGUAGE TRANSLATION**
- *Rule-based system*: Dictionaries + grammatical rules
- *Learning system*: Analyzes millions of sentence pairs in different languages

**THERMOSTAT**
- *Rule-based system*: "7:00 AM → turn on heating, 10:00 PM → turn off"
- *Learning system*: Learns your habits and adjusts temperature

**Human learning analogy:**
- **Rule-based system** = learning to ride a bike from a manual (theory)
- **Learning system** = learning through trial and error (practice)

**Duel results:**
- Simple, predictable tasks: **RULE-BASED SYSTEM WINS**
- Complex, changing problems: **LEARNING SYSTEM WINS**
