Imagine you want to teach someone to play chess. You have two options:

**Approach 1 - Rules:** You write down all possible rules and strategies: "A pawn moves like this...", "If the opponent does this, do that...", "In this situation, always choose this move..."

**Approach 2 - Experience:** You show the basic rules, then let the person play hundreds of games, learning from their own mistakes and successes, discovering strategies independently.

These two approaches represent:
- **Rule-based systems** - operate according to precisely programmed "IF...THEN..." instructions
- **Learning systems** - analyze examples and independently discover patterns

**Key difference:**
- Rule-based systems: "Tell me exactly what to do"
- Learning systems: "Show me examples, and I'll learn myself"

In our daily lives, we encounter both types - from a simple alarm clock (rule: "ring at 7:00 AM") to intelligent Netflix recommendations (learning: "this user likes similar movies").
