
**Narrow AI** (Artificial Narrow Intelligence, ANI) is a system designed to perform ONE specific task. It's like a specialist surgeon - no one will perform heart surgery better, but you wouldn't ask them to treat a broken leg.

**Examples from your life:**
- **Voice assistants** (Siri, Alexa) - recognize speech but don't understand context
- **Spam filters** - separate junk from important emails but won't write you messages
- **Netflix/Spotify** - recommend movies and music but won't choose a restaurant
- **GPS** - will get you to your destination but won't teach you to drive
- **Face recognition** - identifies people but won't read emotions

**Characteristic features:**
- **Super specialization** - excellence in one thing
- **Strict operational framework** - doesn't go beyond programmed functions
- **Lack of true understanding** - performs tasks mechanically
- **Requires training** on specific data
- **Is everywhere** - we use it daily unconsciously

Narrow AI works on the principle of pattern recognition in data. It doesn't "think" like humans but analyzes huge amounts of information and finds regularities in them.
