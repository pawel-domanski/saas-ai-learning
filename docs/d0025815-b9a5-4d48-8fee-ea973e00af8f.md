**Current state (2025):**
All existing AI systems are narrow AI, even the most advanced ones:

- **Language models** (Claude, GPT-4) - specialists in language, without true understanding
- **AlphaFold** - revolutionizes protein prediction but won't write a poem
- **Autonomous cars** - drive great but won't fix the engine

**Why doesn't AGI exist?**
- Lack of true context understanding
- Inability to transfer knowledge between domains
- Lack of self-awareness and creativity
- Limitations to programmed functions

**Expert predictions about AGI:**
- **Optimists**: 10-30 years (technological breakthrough near)
- **Realists**: 30-50 years (significant progress needed)
- **Skeptics**: 50+ years or never (fundamental barriers)

**Why is it so hard to predict?**
- Tasks considered "proof of intelligence" (chess) were conquered by narrow AI
- The bar constantly moves
- We don't fully understand human intelligence
- Unknown technological breakthroughs could change everything

**Significance for the future:**
- **Narrow AI**: gradual improvements in specialized domains
- **General AI**: potentially revolutionary changes in every sphere of life

**Ethical challenges:**
- Narrow AI: control within specialization
- General AI: fundamental questions about control and safety
