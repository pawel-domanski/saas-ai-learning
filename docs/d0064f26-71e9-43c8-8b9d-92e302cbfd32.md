**What are learning systems?**
These are programs that, instead of following rigid rules, analyze data and independently discover patterns. They're like detectives - gathering clues (data) and drawing conclusions.

**How do they work? (credit example):**
1. You get 100,000 historical applications with information: "paid/unpaid"
2. The system analyzes and discovers patterns: "People with high debt and low income more often don't repay"
3. Creates a model predicting repayment probability for new applications
4. Importantly: it can discover non-obvious patterns, e.g., that ZIP code is a better predictor than age!

**Examples from daily life:**
- **Netflix**: Learns your movie preferences
- **Spam filter**: Analyzes millions of emails to recognize spam
- **Google Photos**: Recognizes faces in photos
- **Siri/Alexa**: Learn to understand different accents and ways of speaking
- **Nest thermostat**: Adapts to your habits

**Advantages (+):**
- Adaptability - adjust to new situations
- Discovering non-obvious patterns
- Handling complexity
- Personalization
- Saves programmer work

**Disadvantages (-):**
- "Black box" - hard to understand why it made a decision
- Dependence on data quality
- May learn biases from data
- Needs time and computational power
- Generalization problems
