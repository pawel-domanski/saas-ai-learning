**Myth 1: "AI chatbots are already almost like AGI"**
*Reality*: Language models like Claude can impressively mimic human conversation, but they're still narrow AI systems. They specialize in language but don't have true understanding of the world. It's like an actor playing a doctor - looks convincing but doesn't have medical knowledge.

**Myth 2: "AI is getting smarter and will soon take over"**
*Reality*: Narrow AI is getting better at its specializations, but this doesn't mean approaching general intelligence. It's like comparing an increasingly faster car to the ability to fly - they're completely different categories.

**Myth 3: "AGI is just connecting many narrow AI systems"**
*Reality*: True AGI would require a fundamentally different approach. It's not enough to connect specialists - you need a system that can learn any new skill itself and combine them creatively.

**Myth 4: "AI understands what it does"**
*Reality*: Narrow AI doesn't "understand" in the human sense. It recognizes patterns in data and responds statistically. It has no consciousness, emotions, or intuition.

**How to distinguish fact from fiction:**
- Check information sources
- Remember the difference between narrow and general AI
- Be skeptical of sensational headlines
- Follow expert opinions, not just media

**Why myths are harmful:**
- Create unrealistic expectations
- Can lead to wrong business decisions
- Hinder rational debate about AI's future
- Spread unnecessary fear or excessive optimism

**What to remember:**
- Current AI consists of specialized tools, not thinking beings
- AGI remains a future goal, not current reality
- AI progress is evolution, not overnight revolution
- Healthy skepticism + openness to new = best approach
