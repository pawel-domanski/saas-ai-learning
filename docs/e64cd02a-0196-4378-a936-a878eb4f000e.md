How we define AI today
Why AI doesn't have to think like a human

The modern definition of AI is a system that can:

Interpret external data - understand information from the environment
Learn - improve performance based on experience
Achieve goals - flexibly adapt to situations

Example in practice:
Netflix recommendation system:

Interprets - analyzes what you watch
Learns - understands your preferences
Achieves goal - suggests movies you like

Key shift in thinking:
AI doesn't have to act like a human brain. It can solve problems in a completely different way, achieving better results than humans.
