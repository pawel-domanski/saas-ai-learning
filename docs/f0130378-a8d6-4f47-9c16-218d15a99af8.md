Here are some key principles to keep in mind:
1. You'll be happy to know that each new level is based on what you've learned in the previous ones. Everything related to machine learning is also AI.
2. Hey, just a heads-up: not everything works the other way, and not all AI is machine learning.
3. You'll see that each level has its own special features. That's what makes them different from each other.

Here's a practical example to illustrate what I mean:
*AI: A helpful little tool that can solve equations for you! It follows the same mathematical rules that we do, so it's pretty smart.
ML: Weather Prediction System (learns from historical data).
DL: Face recognition on your iPhone (it uses complex neural networks to do its best for you).
