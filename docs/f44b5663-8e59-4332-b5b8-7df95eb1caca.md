Imagine you have a robot assistant that you want to teach three different tasks:

1. **Recognizing animals in photos** - you show it pictures labeled "cat" or "dog"
2. **Sorting clothes** - you give it a pile of clothes without instructions, let it figure out the categorization
3. **Playing chess** - you let it play and learn from wins and losses

These three scenarios illustrate the three fundamental types of machine learning:

### 🏷️ Supervised Learning
- Like learning with a teacher
- System gets examples with correct answers
- Learns to predict answers for new data

### 🔍 Unsupervised Learning
- Like independent discovery
- System searches for patterns without hints
- Finds groups and structures in data on its own

### 🎮 Reinforcement Learning
- Like learning through experience
- System takes actions and receives rewards or penalties
- Learns strategies that maximize rewards

Each approach has its strengths and best applications. Let's explore them in detail in the following lessons!
