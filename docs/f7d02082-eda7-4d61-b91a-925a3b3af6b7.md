**When to choose RULE-BASED SYSTEMS:**

✅ **Problem is clearly defined**
- Traffic laws, game rules, safety procedures

✅ **You need full control and predictability**
- Nuclear power plant systems, air traffic control

✅ **You have little data**
- New company without transaction history

✅ **Errors are very costly**
- Medical systems where every decision must be justified

✅ **Must be easy to audit**
- Legal systems, financial regulations

**When to choose LEARNING SYSTEMS:**

✅ **Problem is hard to describe with rules**
- Face recognition, natural language translation

✅ **Patterns are subtle and complex**
- Financial fraud detection, sentiment analysis in text

✅ **Data and preferences change**
- Product recommendations, social trends

✅ **You have lots of training data**
- Tech companies with millions of users

✅ **You need personalization**
- User experience, targeted advertising

**Practical decision examples:**

**Bank assessing credit risk:**
- Basic checks: rule-based system (age, income)
- Complex risk assessment: learning system (behavior analysis)

**Autonomous car:**
- Following traffic laws: rule-based system (signs, lights)
- Reacting to unexpected situations: learning system

**E-commerce platform:**
- Basic functions: rule-based system (cart, payments)
- Product recommendations: learning system
